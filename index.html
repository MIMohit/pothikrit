Pothikrit (পথিকৃৎ) is an independent, non-governmental organisation dedicated to promoting digital rights, cyber safety, and legal awareness in Bangladesh.

Our aim is to help people, particularly young people, understand their rights, stay safe online, and become more aware of the laws that shape their everyday lives.

Through awareness programmes, legal research, publications, and community engagement, we strive to make legal knowledge more accessible, empower individuals, and contribute to building a safer and more rights-conscious digital society.

Our work

Areas of focus

Four connected programmes that turn legal knowledge into everyday confidence.

  • Digital Safety

    Practical guidance on privacy, online harassment, scams and account security.

  • Know Your Rights

    Plain-language explainers on the laws that shape everyday life.

  • Research & Publications

    Legal research, policy briefs and reports on digital rights in Bangladesh.

  • Awareness Programmes

    Workshops and campaigns in schools, campuses and communities.
