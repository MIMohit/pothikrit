Questions? info@pothikrit.com

We respect your privacy and collect only the information we need to respond to you.

2.1 What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the details you provide — such as your name, email address, organisation and message.

2.2 How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange programmes you request, or contact you about work you have asked to be involved in. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

2.3 Storage and retention

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to deal with them and for reasonable record-keeping, and take appropriate steps to protect them.

2.4 Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@pothikrit.com.

2.5 Young people

If you are under 18, we encourage you to involve a parent, guardian or trusted adult before sharing personal information with us.
