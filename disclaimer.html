Pothikrit provides legal awareness and information, not necessarily direct legal representation or emergency response. If you are in immediate danger, call 999.

3.1 Not legal advice

Information on this website is general and does not constitute legal advice. Every situation is different; for advice on your specific circumstances, consult a qualified lawyer or contact Government Legal Aid at 16430.

3.2 Accuracy

We aim to keep information accurate and up to date, but laws and procedures change. We make no guarantee that all content is complete or current at the time you read it.

3.3 Helplines

Helpline numbers are listed for convenience and are operated by their respective authorities, not by Pothikrit.
